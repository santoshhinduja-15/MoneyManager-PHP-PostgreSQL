FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to some common questions about MoneyManager.

What is MoneyManager?

MoneyManager is a personal finance management application that helps you record and organize your income and expenses.

Do I need an account to use MoneyManager?

Yes. You need to create an account and log in to manage your personal financial records.

Can I create my own categories?

Yes. You can create and manage your own income and expense categories.

Can I edit or delete my transactions?

Yes. You can view, edit and delete your existing transactions.

Can I use MoneyManager on mobile devices?

Yes. MoneyManager is designed with a responsive interface for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
